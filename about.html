About Career Pilot

A complete full-stack web application designed to bridge the gap between academic education and industry requirements for students and entry-level job seekers.

1. Project Overview & Scope

Many graduating students face uncertainty regarding which career pathway aligns best with their specific programming skills, coursework, and personal problem-solving style. Career Pilot provides an interactive self-assessment platform backed by deterministic, transparent algorithms that evaluate suitability across 10 specialized technology careers.

The system operates 100% locally with zero external API dependencies, ensuring reliability, data privacy, and beginner-friendly maintainability for college project examinations.

2. Transparent Recommendation Formulas

A. Career Match Score Formula (0 to 100%)

Suitability scores are computed through three documented weighted criteria:

Career Score = (Skill Match × 40%) + (Questionnaire Affinity × 35%) + (Profile & Degree Match × 25%)
  • Skill Match (40%): Weighted overlap of possessed skills with career required competencies (High importance = 3 pts, Medium = 2 pts, Low = 1 pt).
  • Questionnaire Affinity (35%): Quantitative alignment with student answers regarding domain excitement, daily tasks, and problem-solving style.
  • Profile & Degree (25%): Relevance of academic specialization, graduation timeline, and career goal keywords.

B. Job Relevance Score Formula (0 to 100%)

Job Relevance = (Skill Overlap × 50%) + (Role Title Match × 25%) + (Location Fit × 15%) + (Experience Fit × 10%)

3. Technology Stack

Backend Engine

  • Python 3 & Flask Framework
  • Flask Blueprints for Modular Routing
  • MySQL Relational Database (15 tables)
  • mysql-connector-python & PyMySQL
  • Werkzeug Password Hashing
  • Native Session-Based Authentication

Frontend Interface

  • Semantic HTML5 & Jinja2 Templates
  • Vanilla CSS3 with Glassmorphic Design
  • Custom CSS Variable Design System
  • Modular Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
  • Zero Heavy External Framework Overhead
